Principal investigator PI · 01

Jiyoung Kang

Associate Professor · Dept. of Scientific Computing, Pukyong National University

Jiyoung Kang leads the Brain Theory Group at PKNU. Trained in theoretical physics at the University of Tsukuba, her work develops dynamic causal models, energy-landscape analyses, and neural-plasticity frameworks that connect biophysical simulation to human neuroimaging data.

  • Mar 2026 – present Associate Professor, Dept. of Scientific Computing, Pukyong National University · Busan, Republic of Korea
  • Sep 2021 – Feb 2026 Assistant Professor, Dept. of Scientific Computing, Pukyong National University · Busan, Republic of Korea
  • Sep 2017 – Aug 2021 Research Professor, Yonsei University College of Medicine, Brain Systems and Brain Science Institute · Seoul, Republic of Korea
  • Apr 2012 – Sep 2017 Project Assistant Professor, Graduate School of Life Science, University of Hyogo · Japan
  • 2009 – 2012 Ph.D., Physics · University of Tsukuba, Japan (JSPS Research Fellowship, DC2)
