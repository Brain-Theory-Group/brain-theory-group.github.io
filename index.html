Computational Brain Science · Network Science · AI

Revealing the principles of brain function through computational theory.

Brain Theory Group (BTG) at Pukyong National University, led by Prof. Jiyoung Kang, develops multiscale and multimodal models of neural dynamics — from single-neuron biophysics to whole-brain effective connectivity.

Research · 04 pillars

What we investigate

Our work bridges biophysical simulation, information theory, and machine learning to describe brain state dynamics at multiple scales.

01  ·  Brain dynamics

Multiscale & multimodal brain dynamics models

Computational models that explain electrophysiology, neuroimaging, genetic, and behavioral data within a shared dynamical framework.

Biologically plausible plasticity for adaptive treatment
